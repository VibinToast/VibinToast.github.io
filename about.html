About Me

I work on retro video games: taking old games apart and putting them back together so they run natively on modern PCs.

What I Do

  • Static recompilation of N64 games to Windows and Linux
  • Modern controls, widescreen and other enhancements for classic games
  • HD texture pack support and tooling

Tools & Platforms

  • N64Recomp and N64ModernRuntime
  • RT64 for rendering
  • Nintendo 64, targeting Windows and Linux

Current Focus

  • Jet Force Gemini: Recompiled, a native PC port of Jet Force Gemini
